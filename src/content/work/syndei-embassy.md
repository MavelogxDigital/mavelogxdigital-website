decisions:
  - title: Service times are a destination, not a footnote
    body: >
      "Plan your visit" sits in the hero with the service times beside it. Sunday and
      Wednesday, 8pm to 9pm GMT. Most church sites bury this in an About page. Putting it in
      the first screen answers the practical question before the theological one.
  - title: WhatsApp is a primary channel, not a social icon
    body: >
      A persistent "Chat us on WhatsApp" control sits bottom-right on every screen. For a Ghanaian congregation WhatsApp is where
      conversation already happens, so the site opens into it instead of a contact form.
  - title: Vertical navigation on the right edge
    body: >
      The primary nav runs vertically down the right-hand side rather than across the top. That frees the full width for the display type and
      the portrait. It is the detail a visitor is most likely to remember.
