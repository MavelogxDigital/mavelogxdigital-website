  Syndei Embassy is a church in Accra. A church website is usually asked to be everything to
  everyone; this one was built around a single question a first-time visitor actually has,
  which is whether they would belong there, and a single action, which is planning a visit.
decisions:
  - title: Service times are a destination, not a footnote
    body: >
      "Plan your visit" sits in the hero with the service times rendered beside it — Sunday and
      Wednesday, 8pm to 9pm GMT. Most church sites bury this in an About page. Putting it in the
      first screen answers the practical question before the theological one.
  - title: WhatsApp is a primary channel, not a social icon
    body: >
      A persistent "Chat us on WhatsApp" control sits bottom-right on every screen. For a
      Ghanaian congregation WhatsApp is where conversation already happens, so the site opens
      into it rather than routing people through a contact form.
  - title: Vertical navigation on the right edge
    body: >
      The primary nav runs vertically down the right-hand side rather than across the top. It
      frees the full width for the display type and the portrait, and it is the single detail a
      visitor is most likely to remember.
