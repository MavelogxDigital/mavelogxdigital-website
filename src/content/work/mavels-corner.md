decisions:
  - title: A positioning line that sets the editorial boundary
    body: >
      "Faith. Hope. Second Chances." sits directly under the masthead. Three words that tell a
      reader what the publication is about and, more usefully for the writer, what it is not —
      which is the thing that keeps a personal blog from becoming a diary.
  - title: Two entry points, two different readers
    body: >
      "Read the Blog" and "Submit a Prayer Request" are given equal weight in the hero. One is
      for a reader, the other for someone arriving in need. Treating them as peers rather than
      making the prayer request a footer link is the editorial decision the design encodes.
  - title: A serif masthead against a sans body
    body: >
      The display type is set in a serif while the interface stays sans. It signals publication
      rather than product, which is the correct register for writing meant to be read slowly.
